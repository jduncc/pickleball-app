Pickleball Round Robin

A simple app for running pickleball round-robin sessions with friends. Add players, auto-pair courts, track scores and standings as you play, and share a live link so everyone can follow along — no spreadsheets, no paper scorecards.

  • Automatic court pairing and fair rotation, for individuals or fixed partners
  • Live scoring and standings everyone on the share link can see in real time
  • Session history with CSV and PDF exports when you're done playing
