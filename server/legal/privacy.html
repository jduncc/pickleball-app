Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

Pickleball Round Robin is a small, self-hosted app for running pickleball round-robin sessions with friends and family. It's run by Jonathan Desrochers for personal, non-commercial use — not a company or a public product. This page explains, in plain terms, what information the app handles.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in. We use Google Sign-In to know who you are. We receive your name and email address from Google — nothing else from your Google account.
  • Session data you enter. Player names, scores, and game history for the round-robin sessions you create or join are stored so the app can function (standings, history, exports).

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, share, or rent your information to anyone.
  • We don't show ads or use third-party trackers or analytics.
  • We don't access any other part of your Google account beyond your name and email.

Who can sign in

This app isn't open to the public. Only Google accounts specifically added to the allow list by the admin can sign in and use it — anyone else's Google sign-in attempt is rejected.

Where your data lives

All data is stored in a single database on the server this app runs on. It isn't shared with any third party. The admin can download a backup of this database and can remove your access (and see your session history) at any time.

Questions or removal requests

If you'd like your account or data removed, or have any questions about this policy, contact the admin at jonathan.desrochers@gmail.com.

Changes

Since this is a small personal project, this policy may be updated occasionally as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date above.
